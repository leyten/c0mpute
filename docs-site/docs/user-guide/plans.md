# Plans

A plan gives you credits every day. There are three.

| | Free | Pro | Max |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | $0 | $12 a month | $30 a month |
| Credits a day | 20 | 300 | 750 |
| Roughly | 20 messages | 300 messages, or 30 images | 750 messages, or 75 images |

Every plan runs the same model with the same features. Vision, thinking, web
search and tools are on everywhere. The plan decides how much you get a day, not
what you get.

## How the daily credits work

Your credits arrive at 00:00 UTC and are gone at the end of the day. They do not
build up. A day you do not use is not saved for later.

The number of messages is a guess, not a rule. A typical message costs about one
credit, but length is the price. Short questions cost less. Pasting a long
document and asking for a long answer costs more. The meter in the chat box
tells you what is left.

When the day's credits run out you are not cut off. Anything further comes out
of your credit balance, so topping up is the way to keep going on a heavy day.

## Buying a plan

Plans are paid for in USDC on Solana. No card, and no credits.

1. Go to [Plans](https://c0mpute.ai/settings#plans) and pick a plan and a
   length. You can buy 1, 3 or 12 months at a time.
2. The page shows the exact amount and your deposit address. Send that amount of
   USDC to it.
3. Press check for payment. The plan starts as soon as the money lands.

A month of Pro is 12 USDC. A month of Max is 30 USDC. Three months of Pro is 36,
a year is 144. The amount on the page is always the whole price of what you
picked.

It is the same address you top up credits with, and the same check. While a
purchase is waiting, USDC arriving there buys the plan instead of credits.

If you send after the quote expires, or send less than the price, the money is
added to your balance as credits at 500 per dollar. Anything over the price
becomes credits too. Money that arrives is never lost and never held.

A purchase you have not paid yet can be cancelled on the Plans page. Picking a
different plan replaces it, so there is only ever one amount to send.

## When the month ends

The plan runs out and you go back to Free. Nothing is taken from your balance,
and there is no subscription to cancel because there was never anything on file.
The Plans page shows the days you have left and sells you another month whenever
you want one.

## Changing plan

**Moving up** happens straight away. You pay the full price of the new plan, and
the time left on the old one comes across as extra days at the price ratio. Half
a month of Pro left when you buy a month of Max is 6 extra days, so you get 36
days of Max for 30 USDC.

**Moving down** waits. There is no refund and nothing to convert downwards, so
the month you paid for runs to the end and then you buy the cheaper plan. The
Plans page will not let you pay for it before then.

Buying the plan you are already on adds months to the end of it.

## Order things are paid for

When you send a message, we spend in this order:

1. Today's plan credits, or your Free daily credits.
2. Your [staking allowance](/staking), if you have one.
3. Your credit balance.

The daily credits go first because they expire. Your balance does not, so
spending it first would throw the day's credits away.

## Without a plan

Signing in gives you the Free plan. There is nothing to buy and no trial that
ends.

You can also use the network without a plan at all, by topping up credits and
paying per message. A typical message costs about a credit, so a few dollars
goes a long way. Plans are cheaper per message, which is why they exist.
